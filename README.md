# Damilola Mustapha

I'm a licensed physician who ended up spending most of his time writing software, and most of what I build falls somewhere between AI agents, on-chain systems and clinical tools.

Seventy-one repositories here, and a little over 2,900 commits authored this year across sixty-five of them. Four of those projects won their hackathon track and a fifth made the round of 16. Deployed across fifteen or so chains, with live mainnet work on multiple EVM networks and Solana.

- **[LEASH](https://leash.ink):** Mission control for the AI agents that spend your money. Bind an existing agent to a governed 2-of-2 co-signed account, declare its spend limits on an ENS name, and cut it off everywhere with one onchain write. ENS + Hedera x402 + Privy.
- **[Triage-0](https://github.com/dmustapha/triage-0):** Won the Psy Models track at Tether's QVAC "Unleash Edge AI" hackathon. Clinical triage for community health workers that runs entirely offline: speech-to-text, reasoning, retrieval and speech all on-device, citing the exact WHO guideline page it used.
- **[Holdline](https://holdline-protect.vercel.app):** Built for Solana Colosseum. Borrow against tokenized stocks without getting liquidated overnight: a repay-only vault plus an autonomous keeper that pays the loan down to a safe buffer while the equity market is closed, and that cannot move the funds anywhere except that repay. Protects obligations on both Kamino KLend and Save.
- **[GhostFund](https://github.com/dmustapha/ghostfund):** Chainlink Convergence 2026 winner. Private DeFi yield vault (Aave V3) with sender privacy and onchain compliance.
- **[AgentAuditor](https://agent-auditor-two.vercel.app):** GoldRush Agentic Track winner. Trust scoring for onchain AI agents: 9-dimension behavioral profiling and onchain attestations across 7 chains.
- **[AgentMesh](https://agentmesh-rho.vercel.app):** 0G sponsor-prize winner at ETHGlobal Open Agents. A peer-to-peer agent mesh with a registry and x402 micropayments, so agents can hire and pay each other without a platform in the middle.
- **[Veil](https://veilzk.vercel.app):** Private cross-chain margin lending. Lock WETH on Ethereum, borrow USDC on Stellar, with a client-side zero-knowledge proof that hides your amount and wallet from the Stellar ledger.
- **[Backstop](https://backstop-alpha.vercel.app):** Veto-only onchain co-signer for autonomous AI agents. Simulates every transaction on live Mantle state and blocks drainers before a single wei can leave.
- **[0G Sentinel / ProofLock](https://sentinel-prooflock.vercel.app):** A provable, revocable admission pass for AI agents on 0G. Seals a verdict onchain that any contract can check in one line.
